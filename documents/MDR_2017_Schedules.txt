### Schedules of the Medical Devices Rules (MDR) 2017 (India)

The Medical Devices Rules, 2017, issued by CDSCO, categorize regulatory requirements into several Schedules. Here is a summary of the most critical schedules:

*   **First Schedule**: Classification of medical devices and in vitro diagnostic (IVD) medical devices. It outlines the parameters and rules for classifying devices into Class A (low risk), Class B (low-moderate risk), Class C (moderate-high risk), and Class D (high risk).
*   **Second Schedule**: Fee structure. Specifies the official government fees required to be paid for various applications, such as import licenses (e.g., $1000 for Class A manufacturing site, $50 per distinct device), manufacturing licenses, test licenses, and clinical investigations.
*   **Third Schedule**: Documents required for registration and licensing. It lists the exact dossier requirements, technical file contents, and plant master file details needed for obtaining import or manufacturing licenses.
*   **Fourth Schedule**: Requirements for the Quality Management System (QMS). It mandates compliance with standards analogous to ISO 13485 for the manufacturing of medical devices and IVDs in India.
*   **Fifth Schedule**: Requirements for conducting clinical investigations. It dictates the protocols, ethics committee approvals, and reporting formatting required for trials of investigational medical devices in human subjects.
*   **Sixth Schedule**: Post-Market Surveillance (PMS) and exemptions.
*   **Seventh Schedule**: Requirements for clinical performance evaluation of new in vitro diagnostic (IVD) medical devices.
*   **Eighth Schedule**: Exemption of specific medical devices from the provisions of the rules.

These schedules form the backbone of regulatory compliance for both domestic manufacturing and importing medical devices into the Indian market.
