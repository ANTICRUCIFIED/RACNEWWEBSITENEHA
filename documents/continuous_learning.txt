User Query: What is CDSCO MD-14?
RAAAHI Expert Answer: In India, **Form MD-14** is the official application submitted to the Central Licensing Authority (CDSCO) to obtain an **Import Licence** for bringing medical devices (Class A, B, C, or D) into the country under the Medical Devices Rules (MDR), 2017. 

Here is a summary of how Form MD-14 works and its key requirements:

---

### Key Aspects of Form MD-14:

1. **Who Applies?**  
   The application is submitted online via the **CDSCO SUGAM Portal** by an Authorized Indian Agent or an authorized importer who holds a valid local Wholesale Drug/Device Licence (Form 20B/21B).

2. **Outcome (Licence Grant):**  
   Upon successful review and approval of the MD-14 dossier by CDSCO, the official Import Licence is issued in **Form MD-15**.

3. **Government Fee Structure for MD-14:**
   * **Class A:** $1,000 USD per overseas manufacturing site + $50 USD per distinct medical device.
   * **Class B:** $2,000 USD per overseas manufacturing site + $1,000 USD per distinct medical device.
   * **Class C:** $3,000 USD per overseas manufacturing site + $1,500 USD per distinct medical device.
   * **Class D:** $3,000 USD per overseas manufacturing site + $1,500 USD per distinct medical device.

4. **Required Documentation:**  
   The application requires a comprehensive dossier, including the Power of Attorney (PoA), Device Master File (DMF), Plant Master File (PMF), Free Sale Certificate (FSC) from the country of origin, quality management system certificates (ISO 13485), and test reports.

---

### Reference Resource:
You can review the official requirements and document structure in the [CDSCO Form MD-14 CSF Guidance Document PDF](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_Host/pdf-documents/medical-device/Guidance_Document_CSF_Import_Licence.pdf).
