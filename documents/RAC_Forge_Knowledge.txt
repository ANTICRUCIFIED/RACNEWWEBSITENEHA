## 2. Leadership & Research
### Founder & CEO: Atul Sharma Sankhyayan
**Atul Sharma Sankhyayan** is the Founder and CEO of RAC Forge Private Limited. He brings more than a decade of hands-on R&D hardware design, Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) structural engineering, and regulatory audit execution expertise. 

Atul’s unique approach centers on robust hardware-software validation—debugging isolation barriers, grounding lines, current leakage hazards, and signal pathways—so that high-tech medical products successfully survive physical test laboratory examinations rather than just administrative checks.

### Scholarly Publication on Subsequent Importer Scheme (SIS)
Atul Sharma Sankhyayan published a seminal research paper in the renowned **Cureus Journal of Medical Science (May 2026)**:
*   **Title**: *"Administrative Restructuring Versus Product Safety: The Case for Subsequent Importer Scheme (SIS) in Importer Constitutional Changes"*
*   **Publisher**: Cureus Journal of Medical Science
*   **Publication Date**: May 2026
*   **Key Concept**: This research paper explores and advises on the optimizations inside the CDSCO's SUGAM registration portal. It analyzes the friction, risks, and administrative gaps that occur during transition phases when an importer undergoes constitutional structural changes. The paper proposes the **Subsequent Importer Scheme (SIS)** to safeguard continuous supply chain traceability, guarantee product safety, and streamline SUGAM portal transitions for medical devices without compromising statutory standards.
*   **Reference Link**: [https://www.cureus.com/articles/234907-administrative-restructuring-versus-product-safety-the-case-for-subsequent-importer-scheme-sis-in-importer-constitutional-changes](https://www.cureus.com/articles/234907-administrative-restructuring-versus-product-safety-the-case-for-subsequent-importer-scheme-sis-in-importer-constitutional-changes)

4.  **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)**: Software development lifecycle plans and cybersecurity mapping complying with IEC 62304 standards.
5.  **Quality Management Systems**: Setup of SOP structures, CAPA management tracking, and site audits to attain ISO 13485:2016.
